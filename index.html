01 · Payments & platform leadership

Sentinel 360

Demonstrates operational product judgment: distinguish the initiating fault from downstream symptoms and choose a defensible recovery action.

Try: Find a dependency failure, compare recovery options, then check whether service health actually recovers.

The product decision
Customer & problem
Payment reliability engineers reconciling conflicting alerts during an incident.
Why consider investment?
Misdiagnosis can delay recovery or trigger an unnecessary change. Test whether an evidence-led workflow improves decision accuracy before building integrations.
Alternative & tradeoff
Manual dashboard/log correlation is flexible. The constrained service model makes reasoning visible but covers fewer production failure modes.
Measure success
Correct initiating-service identification, time to a justified decision, and unnecessary recovery actions in paired tasks. These are proposed measures, not achieved results.

02 · Agent governance & execution controls

AgentShield

Demonstrates governance product judgment: bind consent to the exact action and recheck approval at execution time.

Try: Approve a purchase, change a policy or advance the clock, then try to execute the original request.

The product decision
Customer & problem
Agent-platform owners and reviewers managing permissions and stale consent.
Why consider investment?
Approval that survives a request or policy change can permit unintended actions. Validate the failure pattern and review burden before proposing an enforcement service.
Alternative & tradeoff
Broad permissions reduce friction; manual review adds control but can become a bottleneck. Request-bound approval adds safeguards and additional review work.
Measure success
Unauthorized fixture execution, stale-approval rejection, reviewer task completion, and review burden. No real access enforcement is claimed.
Open AgentShield →Working approval simulation · no production enforcement
03 · AI platforms & technical product depth

ModelForge Lab

Demonstrates AI product judgment: separate training objectives from adaptation methods and expose resource assumptions before selecting an experiment.

Try: Compare LoRA and DoRA parameters, inspect memory subtotals, then train a tiny model and compare held-out loss.

The product decision
Customer & problem
AI product and engineering teams comparing adaptation options with incomplete resource estimates.
Why consider investment?
A mistaken compute or data assumption can derail an experiment. Test whether the lab improves method selection and estimate comprehension.
Alternative & tradeoff
Documentation and notebooks offer depth. A browser lab lowers setup effort but omits actual GPU profiling and LLM training.
Measure success
Correct method/data matching, explanation of excluded memory components, and quality of an experiment plan—not an assumed model-quality gain.
Open ModelForge Lab →Real tiny-model training · analytical LLM estimates
