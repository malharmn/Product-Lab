Three kinds of evidence, clearly separated

1 · Working behavior

State transitions, calculations and acceptance checks show how a constrained workflow behaves under synthetic inputs.

2 · Product reasoning

Each brief identifies a proposed customer, current alternative, MVP, tradeoff and the next validation decision.

3 · Observed user tests

A local research log records actual task observations when tests are conducted. No testimonials or outcomes are prefilled.
